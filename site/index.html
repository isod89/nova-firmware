Your ATS Mini,
reimagined.

NOVA is a modern, complete and open source firmware for the ATS Mini pocket receiver. Install it from your browser in one minute, and go back whenever you want.

Install NOVA Download Source code

Every band

FM, AM, longwave and shortwave, SSB and CW, with a scanner that finds every station and names them with RDS and the EiBi schedule.

Three apps

A professional Ghost Box, a Noise Hunter that finds what spoils your reception, and a Storm Detector that hears lightning.

Made to look good

19 themes, an animated boot screen, smooth fonts and six visualizers driven by the receiver's own measurements.

A real editor

Stations, names, settings, backups, updates and app control panels, in a web page that works offline over USB.

Safe to try

The installer backs up the original firmware first and restores it in a minute.

Free, forever

GPL-3.0. Every line of the firmware, the editor and the installer is public.

Ghost Box, done properly

Fast sweeps from 30 ms, six sweep orders, clusters around stations, RF trigger, catch, answer windows and blind sessions. Every frequency is logged, and the editor records, shows a spectrogram, checks each response against the stations on the air, and prints a report.

A real control panel

The NOVA Editor runs in your browser, offline, over the USB cable: rename stations, change every setting, update station names over Wi-Fi, back up, update, and drive the three apps with live charts. Try the demo.

Install in one minute

NOVA Installer reads your radio, picks the right build, saves the original firmware and installs NOVA, with every part checked. Going back is one click. Read the tutorials.

19 themes

From the deep blue NOVA theme to Night red for the observatory, Retro LCD, Paper, Nord, Dracula and a high contrast theme for bright sunlight.
